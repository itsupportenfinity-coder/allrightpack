# Business Rules

Goal:

Generate WhatsApp enquiries.

Every improvement must increase one of:

- Trust
- Speed
- Readability
- Lead generation
- Ease of ordering

Never sacrifice usability for aesthetics.

Target users:

Factories
Restaurants
Hotels
Wholesale buyers

Age:

35–65

Priority:

Trust > Fancy design

Fast enquiry > Fancy animation

Professional > Trendy

Clear > Complex
Priority Order

1. Trust
2. Ease of Ordering
3. Speed
4. Readability
5. Responsiveness
6. SEO
7. Performance
8. Animations
9. Visual Polish

Never increase visual complexity.

Whenever there are two possible solutions:

Choose the simpler one.

If an animation doesn't improve usability, remove it.
